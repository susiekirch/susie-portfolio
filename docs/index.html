02 / About

A creative mind with a practical streak.

I see the connections between ideas, systems, content, design, and the person on the other side of the screen.

My work moves across creative strategy, brand direction, content and publishing, visual design, digital products, WordPress, creator education, AI-assisted workflows, and storytelling.

I like the part where something is still messy: listening closely, finding its shape, making a plan, and carrying it through to a useful result.
