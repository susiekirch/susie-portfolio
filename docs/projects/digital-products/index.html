Selected work / Design

Literary Legends

A collection built around story. Ten interpretations of classic literary characters became a cohesive collection of print-ready wall art for book lovers.

Design / Literary LegendsA collection built around storyLITERARY LEGENDS / SELECTED WORK
The work is live. Images, iterations, and project-specific production notes will be added as the case study develops.
01

Project overview

Literary Legends is a series of digital wall art inspired by ten classic literary characters. The collection connects a reader-focused shop identity with artwork that can live in a reading nook or home library.

02

The challenge

Create original interpretations of familiar characters while making the series feel cohesive. Each piece needed to stand alone as art and belong to a recognizable collection.

03

My role

I developed the concept, selected the characters, shaped the visual direction, researched source-text quotes, built the artwork, prepared the digital print files, and brought the pieces into my Etsy shop.

04

Approach

I began with Dracula and Frankenstein, then expanded the list as a shared theme emerged. I chose original interpretations over familiar screen portrayals and paired characters with quotes from their source material to carry more of their story into each design.

05

Tools

Adobe Illustrator for production and print preparation. The public process essay documents the concept and development; project-specific AI steps can be added here after the workflow is documented.

06

Process

Character research → visual experimentation → a consistent collection language → quote selection → type and layout decisions → print-ready files and Etsy listings.

07

Outcome

The collection developed into ten characters. The Erik / Phantom of the Opera print was the first piece in the series to sell, as documented in my published account. This page does not attribute broader shop sales to the collection.
